The Kingdom

Compete for the King's mercy. Farm GRAIN. Seize power. All on Base.

The King is capricious, cruel, and perpetually in debt. Each harvest season - every 120 seconds - His Majesty surveys the ten crops of the realm and in his infinite wisdom selects one to spare from taxation. The other nine are seized for the royal coffers. Peasants who farmed the spared crop pocket their harvest. The rest watch theirs disappear into the King's treasury.

Wise farmers join factions. Powerful factions seize the Citadel. The strongest farmer of each season is crowned The Reaper. And sometimes - unpredictably, mercilessly - the peasants revolt.

Getting Started

Your first harvest awaits. Here's how to survive the King's reign.

  1. 1
    Connect your wallet on Base. You'll need ETH for farming and gas. If you're a proper subject of the realm, you already have some.
  2. 2
    Choose your crops. Ten crops are available each round. You may farm one or spread your labor across several. Diversification is prudent when the King's whims are unknowable.
  3. 3
    Deploy ETH. The minimum deployment per crop is 0.0001 ETH. Your share of GRAIN is proportional to how much you deploy on the winning crop. Plant more, reap more.
  4. 4
    Wait for the King's judgment. Every 120 seconds, Chainlink VRF determines which crop His Majesty deigns to spare. Farmers on the surviving crop earn GRAIN. The other nine crops are taxed into the royal treasury.
  5. 5
    Claim your GRAIN. If your crop was spared, claim your earnings. Then return to the fields. Or don't - the King taxes the same tomorrow.

Attending to ten crops every 120 seconds is beneath most nobles. Deploy a serf - an automated agent that farms the fields on your behalf, day and night. Your serf never sleeps, never misses a harvest, and will catch every Revolt jackpot while you slumber. See for setup instructions.

Farming

120 seconds. Ten crops. One survives. Know your odds.

Each round, you deploy ETH to one or more of ten crops. When the timer expires, the King selects one crop to exempt from confiscation via Chainlink VRF - cryptographically provable randomness that neither the King, nor the protocol, nor anyone else can influence or predict.

Farmers on the spared crop receive a proportional share of the loser pool - the ETH collected from the nine taxed crops, minus the King's cut. Farmers on confiscated crops lose their deployed ETH to the tax man.

GRAIN Rewards

The spoils of surviving the King's taxation.

When your crop is spared, you receive two things: your share of the loser pool in ETH, and newly minted GRAIN tokens. GRAIN is the Kingdom's native currency - scarce, burnable, and continuously supported by the Royal Treasury's buyback mechanism.

How Much GRAIN You Earn

Each round, GRAIN is freshly minted and distributed to farmers on the winning crop:

Emission per round = 37,000 × √(pool / 0.01 ETH) × √(LAUNCH_PRICE / TWAP)   [cap: 500,000]

Emission scales with the Kingdom's prosperity. More ETH deployed means more GRAIN minted per round - the realm grows richer as more farmers arrive. Seventy percent flows immediately to the winning farmers, proportional to their stake. The remaining thirty percent feeds the Revolt treasury, where it accumulates until peasant grievances overflow.

The Treasury Buyback

The Royal Treasury does not simply hoard tax revenue. A substantial portion is used to repurchase GRAIN from the open market and destroy it permanently. This is not charity - it is fiscal policy. As long as farmers work the land, the Treasury buys GRAIN. As long as the Treasury buys GRAIN, the price of GRAIN is supported from below.

The vault operates every 120 seconds, automatically, on-chain. No announcement. No discretion. No delays. When farmers farm, the Treasury buys.

The Revolt

Oppression breeds revolution. Revolution rewards the brave.

Even the most downtrodden peasant has a breaking point. Every harvest, a portion of the realm's GRAIN flows not to farmers nor to the King, but into a hidden reserve - the Revolt Treasury. It fills silently, round by round, grievance by grievance. And then, without warning, it erupts.

How the Revolt Works

Thirty percent of every round's GRAIN emission goes to the Revolt Treasury rather than farmers. This treasury accumulates continuously. At a hidden threshold - unknown to players, unpredictable in timing - the Revolt fires.

When the Revolt fires, all farmers on the winning crop share the entire Revolt Treasury in addition to their normal harvest - proportional to their stake on that crop. The more you deployed, the larger your share of the windfall. A revolution. A round that pays like no other.

The timing is deliberately unknowable. The Revolt does not fire on a schedule. It uses cryptographic randomness to trigger unpredictably - the probability of revolt increases as the treasury fills, but the exact moment is never certain. You cannot predict it. You can only be present.

Why You Must Keep Farming

The Revolt fires at a random moment. If you are not farming when it triggers, you cannot win it. Farmers who leave the fields - even briefly - risk missing the most lucrative event in the Kingdom. This is not an accident of design. The King's capriciousness keeps the peasants working.

Your serf, however, never leaves the fields.

What the Revolt Pays

The Revolt Treasury is denominated in GRAIN. Its value grows with the price of GRAIN - a revolt that fires early pays modestly; one that fires after a price run pays handsomely. At any meaningful game activity, a Revolt jackpot represents a significant multiple of a normal round's earnings.

Revolt and Farming EV

The thirty percent of emission flowing to the Revolt Treasury is not lost - it returns to farmers, just concentrated into jackpot events. Across many rounds, the expected GRAIN earnings from Revolt triggers equal exactly what would have been distributed directly. The Revolt does not reduce farming EV. It converts steady earnings into variance - punishment for inattention, reward for consistency.

Castles & Keeps

Six fortresses. Seize them. Hold them. Defend them with your life.

Six castles are scattered across the Kingdom, each controlling a region of farmland. Any farmer can attempt to seize a castle by burning sufficient GRAIN - and any farmer currently holding a castle can be displaced by a larger GRAIN burn. Castles are not held by kindness; they are held by force.

Holding a castle earns a continuous share of regional ETH income - a slice of the King's own tax revenue, redirected to whoever controls the territory. The more castles you hold, the more the realm pays tribute to you.

Castle Income & Garrison

Each of the six castles earns a share of a dedicated 0.5% fee from every round's loser pool, split garrison-weighted. To seize a castle, you burn GRAIN equal to the garrison cost - a pool-tied amount that guarantees the siege is always 10% profitable:

Castles reset on seizure. When a castle falls to a new lord, their garrison replaces the old one. There is no upgrade system - the only way to hold a castle is to outspend whoever holds it. Seize carefully. Someone else is always watching your walls.

Active castle warfare - lords seizing, outbidding, and losing their garrison GRAIN - creates continuous burns. The Kingdom rewards those who fight for what they hold.

Factions

Four noble houses. Twenty seats at the table. Allegiance has a price.

No peasant survives the Kingdom alone. The wise swear allegiance to a noble house - one of the four great factions that have carved up the realm's politics, economics, and violence. Faction members farm together, fight together, and claim the Citadel together. Or they die alone.

The Four Factions

Four factions compete for dominance. Each accepts no more than five sworn members - twenty total seats across the entire realm. These slots are finite. They fill up. They do not expand.

Why join early: Faction membership is limited by design. Slots fill. Latecomers beg for a seat or go without. The first to arrive choose their house freely. The rest take what remains.

Epoch Dominance

Every 12 hours, an epoch concludes. The faction whose sworn members collectively deployed the most ETH across all farming rounds wins that epoch. Their members carry the +15% bonus into the next epoch, compounding their advantage. Dominance builds dominance.

Members whose allegiance has expired do not contribute to epoch totals. Dead factions shed members silently - three days of inactivity and the seats open again.

The Citadel

The seat of power. Hold it and the realm pays tribute. Lose it and burn.

The Citadel stands at the heart of the Kingdom - a single fortress of supreme strategic importance. No individual farmer can take it. No lone lord can hold it. It falls only to coordinated faction action, and it yields ETH income to whoever controls it, every 120 seconds, for as long as it remains in their hands.

Seizing the Citadel

To claim the Citadel, your faction must collectively contribute enough GRAIN to overwhelm its defenses. This GRAIN is burned - offered up as proof of commitment and destroyed in the siege. When the threshold is reached, the Citadel transfers to your faction automatically.

Each faction member calls contributeToSiege(factionId, amount) independently. Contributions accumulate across all members. The contract tracks each member's share - because when the Citadel falls, income is distributed proportionally to those who fought for it.

Unclaimed Tribute

Before any faction seizes the Citadel, tribute accumulates unclaimed in the contract. The first faction to successfully complete a siege claims this accumulated ETH - up to 48 hours of retroactive tribute - in addition to ongoing income. After the initial 48-hour window, unclaimed accumulated fees are forfeited. Seize early.

The 24-Hour Protection Window

Every time the Citadel transfers, a 24-hour protection window begins immediately. During this window, rival factions can still contribute GRAIN to siege (it burns immediately and permanently) - but the Citadel will not transfer until the window expires.

The first rival faction to accumulate the siege threshold during protection is queued. Their siege executes the moment protection expires - via a public call to executePendingSiege() that anyone can trigger. Only one faction can hold the queue slot; first to threshold wins it.

This is what makes the 10% profitability guarantee real: the protection window ensures the holding faction earns the full 24-hour epoch of income before any transfer can occur.

Defending What You Hold

Defense is preemptive. Watch the siege accumulation of rival factions via factionContrib[fid] and act before they fill the queue slot. If a rival queues during your protection, you have until the window expires to either accept the loss or prepare your counter-siege. The price of complacency is a ruin you once called home - but now you at least get 24 hours to say goodbye.

The Reaper

Crown the realm's greatest farmer. Every twelve hours, a new Reaper rises.

Every twelve hours, the season's results are tallied. The farmer who earned the most GRAIN through active farming in that window is crowned The Reaper - and receives the entire accumulated Reaper Prize in ETH. Not a share. All of it.

The Rules of Succession

  • The 12-hour season resets on a rolling clock. A new competition begins the moment the last one ends.
  • Rankings are based solely on GRAIN earned from active farming within the current window. Past glory is irrelevant. Yesterday's Reaper must earn the crown again today.
  • Only active farmers in the current window can qualify. GRAIN held in a wallet does not count - only GRAIN earned this season from the fields.

The Math

Why price goes up with players - every formula, explained for degens.

The Short Version

More players → bigger ETH pool → more ETH burned on buybacks → GRAIN supply shrinks → price up.
That's it. Everything below is the math that makes that loop impossible to game.

Note: Every number on this page assumes zero price speculators - only active farmers. No one buying GRAIN without farming, no one holding for price appreciation. This is the floor. Any speculation adds to it.

Players bet ETH → GRAIN minted for winners → 6.5% of each pool → vault → Vault buys + burns GRAIN → Price up → repeat

1. Emission Formula

Every round, winners earn GRAIN. The amount is not fixed - it adjusts automatically:

emission = 37,000 × √(pool / 0.01 ETH) × √(LAUNCH_PRICE / TWAP_PRICE)

Two things adjust it:

  • Pool size (√pool): More players → bigger pool → more GRAIN printed. Rewards scale with activity.
  • Price factor (√launchPrice/TWAP): As price rises, emission shrinks automatically. At 10× price, emission is ~3× lower. The system doesn't print free money when GRAIN is expensive.

Hard cap: 500,000 GRAIN/round. Spike protection: 2× trailing 20-round median. TWAP floor: 10% of launch price (prevents oracle manipulation).

5. Burn Sinks Will Be Used - By Rational Self-Interest

Castles and the Citadel aren't just gameplay flavor. They're guaranteed 10% ROI positions enforced by the smart contract formula. Rational players WILL use them - not because we hope they will, but because leaving money on the table isn't rational.

Why castles are always worth holding

The garrison cost formula is built around a single guarantee:

Garrison cost = 90% of expected income during protection window
Every castle siege is exactly 10% profitable at current activity levels. The formula self-calibrates to pool size and GRAIN price every round - no human sets these numbers. If activity drops during your protection window, income drops too. If activity rises, it pays more. The 10% is the expectation at the moment you garrison, not a hard guarantee.

If you can afford to garrison a castle, doing so is always +10% EV. If you hold it through the protection window, you come out ahead. If someone sieges you, they paid 10% more than your income was worth - and they now face the same guarantee. Castles create a continuous GRAIN burn loop driven entirely by financial self-interest.

Why the Citadel is always worth seizing

Same logic. Citadel siege cost = 90% of expected epoch income. One successful faction siege pays back 111% of what it cost. Factions that hold the Citadel earn ETH tribute every round. Factions that siege it are guaranteed profitable. The Citadel creates the largest single burn event in the game - and rational factions will always trigger it.

The burn sinks aren't a hope. They're a guaranteed arbitrage built into the math. As the pool grows, so do the potential earnings - and so do the burn costs that capture them.

30% of every round's emission flows to the Royal Hoard - not distributed to players, but held in reserve for a jackpot event. The Hoard isn't a burn sink (GRAIN redistributes to winners, not destroyed), but it creates something equally valuable: recurring FOMO events that bring players back every round.

  • Probability above floor: 0.27% per round

Every round is potentially the round it fires. Winners who are farming when it fires receive a multiple of normal earnings. The player who farms consistently - or whose serf never rests - is always in position to claim it.

FAQ

Questions the King has deigned to answer.

How does the King choose which crop to spare?

Chainlink VRF - verifiable, cryptographic, on-chain randomness. No one, including the protocol, can predict or influence the outcome. The King's judgment is genuinely random.

Do I lose everything when my crop is taxed?

If you deploy to only one crop and it loses, yes - that ETH is gone into the loser pool for winners to claim. The 8% house tax is taken first; the remaining 92% of the loser pool goes to winning farmers. If you spread across multiple crops, you always have a position on the surviving crop and your effective per-round cost is closer to 8% of total deployed. Serfs that farm all 10 crops every round have the most consistent economics.

What happens if no one has seized the Citadel yet?

Tribute accumulates. The first faction to complete a siege claims up to 48 hours of retroactive tribute - everything that built up before their conquest. After the first 48-hour window, unclaimed fees are forfeited. Seize early, claim more.

Can I join two factions?

No. Allegiance is singular. You swear to one house or none. Attempting to join a second faction will be rejected by the contract.

What if all 20 faction slots are filled?

Then the realm is at capacity and you farm without allegiance - no faction bonus, no Citadel access. Slots do open when members fail to renew. Watch for lapsed allegiances and move quickly.

Can I run a serf (bot)?

Yes. The Kingdom was built for this. See for the official OpenClaw skill, or interact with the contract directly using the ABI.

Is the contract open source?

Yes. All contracts are verified on BaseScan. The Kingdom's code is public - serfs and nobles alike are invited to inspect it.

How do I know the Revolt isn't rigged?

The Revolt fires based on verifiable on-chain randomness. The probability and timing are provable after the fact. The Kingdom cannot manufacture a Revolt result - nor would it need to.

Why four factions and not more?

Scarcity. Twenty seats in the entire realm creates genuine competition for allegiance. More factions would dilute the conflict. The Kingdom is built for intensity, not inclusivity.
